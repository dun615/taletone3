DOLONGCHA 2nd Single 'Bubble Sweet' Released

Virtual artist DOLONGCHA's second original single, 'Bubble Sweet,' with music production by TALETONE, has been released.

On a sunlit summer day, a small and sweet story begins inside a clear cup.

Inspired by bubble tea, popping boba, and a variety of cafe drinks, 'Bubble Sweet' pairs a melody that flows like a journey through a colorful drink with the effervescent thrill of excitement, brought to life by DOLONGCHA's lovely, pure voice.

TALETONE was involved across the full production of the song, from overall music production to lyric writing, composition, arrangement, synthesizer, drum programming, and bass guitar performance.

Set against a buoyant rhythm and an addictive hook, the song captures the moment when a sip of sweetness turns into a small happiness. We hoped the tender feelings rising like bubbles would follow the music all the way to a clear summer sky.

We are sincerely grateful to have joined this precious and delightful story.
We hope 'Bubble Sweet' stays with you as a sweet, magical moment throughout your summer.
